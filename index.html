Our story

Namak Mandi is a love letter to Peshawar — its food and its people, its old city and its open fires, its gentle spices and its great gatherings.

In 2010, the true cooking of Peshawar was hard to find in London. The food of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is milder than that of Lahore or Karachi — cumin, coriander, cardamom and black pepper; green chilli, ginger and garlic — and proud of its restraint.

So we carried our family recipes to Upper Tooting Road and cooked them without shortcuts: spices ground by hand, nothing pre-cooked or pre-packed, karahi meeting the flame only when you order. Fifteen years on, the room is still busy, the fire is still lit, and every dish is still a letter home.

Food, drink & allergens

Before you order

◆

Our kitchen handles milk, gluten (wheat in naan and breads), nuts, sesame, eggs, fish, mustard, celery and soya, and many dishes share pans and fryers — so we cannot promise any dish is completely free of an allergen. Talk to us first; we will always check a dish's ingredients with you.

Allergies & intolerancesTell us when you book, order or are seated. Our team will confirm the ingredients of the dishes you choose.
VegetarianMarked (V) on the menu, with a filter above. Vegetarian dishes are cooked in our shared kitchen.
HalalAll of our meat is halal and HMC approved. We do not serve alcohol, and alcohol cannot be brought in.
HeatPeshawari cooking is gently spiced — ask and we will happily make a dish milder, or hotter.
